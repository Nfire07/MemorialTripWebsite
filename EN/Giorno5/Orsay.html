D’Orsay museum

From train stations to a museum of impressionism

The Musée d’Orsay is a museum in Paris housed in a former railway station, famous for its extraordinary collection of Impressionist and Post-Impressionist works, including masterpieces by Monet, Van Gogh, and Degas.

Recommended duration: 5 hours • Access with booked ticket • Free photography

Main stages

Stage 1

The train station (Gare d'Orsay)

The museum was born from a former railway station from the late 19th century, converted into an exhibition space: the large nave with the monumental clock retains the original industrial atmosphere, making the architecture an integral part of the artistic experience.

Stage 2

Vincent van Gogh

The museum houses some of Van Gogh's most famous masterpieces, such as The Bedroom at Arles and Starry Night Over the Rhone, offering a profound insight into his expressive painting and his transition to modern art.
