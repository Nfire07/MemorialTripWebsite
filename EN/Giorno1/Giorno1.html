Proposed Itinerary

Approximate duration: 4–5 hours total

  • Departure — B&B HOTEL Paris Porte de la Villette (4 Rue Émile Reynaud)
  • 🚇 Metro 7: Porte de la Villette → Stalingrad (4 stops, ~5 min)
  • 👣 Transfer (4 min) at Stalingrad
  • 🚇 Metro 2: Stalingrad → Anvers (3 stops, ~4 min)
  • ⛪ Basilica of Sacré-Cœur — Arrival and visit
  • 🚶 Walk (15 min) to the Moulin Rouge

Useful Info

  • Transport: Metro 2 (Anvers), 7 (Stalingrad)
  • Accessibility: steep stairs in Montmartre; funicular available
  • Clothing: comfortable shoes, water, shoulder cover for the basilica
