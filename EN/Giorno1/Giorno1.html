Proposed Itinerary

Approximate duration: 4–5 hours total

  • Departure — B&B HOTEL Paris Porte de la Villette (4 Rue Émile Reynaud)
  • Walk → Aimé Césaire (M12) — ~19 min
  • M12 Aimé Césaire → Abbesses (direction “Mairie d’Issy”) — 7 stops
  • Walk Abbesses → Montmartre — ~9 min
  • Montmartre tour: Place du Tertre, Saint-Pierre-de-Montmartre, view from Sacré-Cœur
  • Visit Basilica of Sacré-Cœur (exterior + interior)
  • Metro to Tuileries (line 12 → Concorde, then M1 towards Château de Vincennes)
  • Tuileries Garden and Palace — explanation of the symbolism of the May 23, 1871 fire
  • Walk to Hôtel de Ville — ~15 min
  • Hôtel de Ville — seat of the Commune Council, comparison with current function

Useful Info

  • Transport: Metro 2 (Anvers), 12 (Abbesses)
  • Accessibility: steep stairs in Montmartre; funicular available
  • Clothing: comfortable shoes, water, shoulder cover for the basilica
