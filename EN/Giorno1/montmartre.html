Recommended Route (2–3 hours)

Start from Wall of "I Love You" → Bateau-Lavoir → Picasso's First Studio → Place du Tertre → Saint-Pierre → Sacred Heart.

Practical Info

Comfortable shoes, water, and raincoat if needed. Inside the basilica: respectful behaviour.

  • Access: metro Abbesses/Anvers; funicular to the Sacred Heart.
  • Stop Duration: 10–20′ per stop.
  • Materials: 1–2 historical images for Commune explanation (optional).
